Which nut and washer go with which bolt
BoltNutWasher
A325 structural (F3125 Gr A325)A563 Gr DH heavy hex (A563 C or DH for plain)F436 hardened
A490 structuralA563 Gr DH heavy hexF436 hardened
A307 / Grade 2 hex boltGrade A hex nutUSS or SAE flat
Grade 5 hex boltGrade 5 hex nutUSS / SAE flat
Grade 8 hex boltGrade 8 hex nutGrade 8 through-hardened flat
F1554 anchor bolt / B7 rodA194 2H or A563 heavy hexUSS flat or plate washer

Hot-dip galvanized bolts need hot-dip galvanized nuts. The nuts are tapped oversize to clear the zinc, so a plain or zinc-plated nut will not thread on.

When you add a bolt to your order list, we suggest the matching nut and washer in the same size and finish.
